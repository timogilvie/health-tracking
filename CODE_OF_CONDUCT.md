# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, precise, and constructive.
- Respect differing experiences and technical viewpoints.
- Accept correction gracefully and focus criticism on the work.
- Protect other people's privacy. Never request or repost personal health information.
- Avoid medical claims, diagnosis, or treatment advice in project support channels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub private
security-advisory channel when privacy is involved. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject
contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and recused.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
