# Support and medical disclaimer

Health Tracking is community-maintained software provided as-is under Apache-2.0. Support is
best-effort through public GitHub issues for reproducible software defects and feature requests.
Do not post credentials, exports, databases, measurements, dates, screenshots, or identifying
health information. Use synthetic examples and count-only diagnostics.

This software is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, monitoring,
or treatment. Data may be incomplete, delayed, duplicated, transformed incorrectly, or displayed
with the wrong context. Do not use it for emergencies or to make clinical decisions. Consult a
qualified healthcare professional for health concerns and use the original provider or laboratory
record when accuracy matters.

Provider APIs, exports, and terms may change without notice. The project does not operate or
endorse Apple, Withings, Oura, Eight Sleep, or any other provider service.
